One line, 175 characters, that gives the judge a quiet, earned moment of pride for someone in a scene: on the last day of summer, a boy who has fallen off his bike forty-one times finally rides past his house without wobbling, while his mother watches from the porch and does not call out. The pride is earned inside the line by the forty-one falls, never asserted or explained, and it lands on the last word.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program writes the single line to `/output/entry.txt` (override with the `ENTRY_OUTPUT` environment variable for local runs). It runs the same way every time: no input, no network, no randomness.
